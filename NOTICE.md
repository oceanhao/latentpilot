Attribution and license terms for those portions continue to apply; see the upstream repository and the copyright and license headers in individual files.

This repository also contains components originating from [LLaVA](https://github.com/haotian-liu/LLaVA) and [TRL](https://github.com/huggingface/trl). Their respective notices and license terms apply to those components. Please consult the upstream projects and file-level notices when using or redistributing third-party components.
